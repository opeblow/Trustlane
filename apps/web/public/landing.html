From “find it” to “buy it”—with you in control.

Trustlane makes an agent’s shopping decisions understandable, policy-aware, and reviewable before payment.

01 — REQUEST

Understand what you asked for

Turn everyday instructions into structured budgets, product constraints, and approval requirements.

02 — POLICY

Check every decision

Evaluate price, category, merchant, quote freshness, and spending limits with deterministic rules.

03 — PAYMENT

Ask before money moves

Bind approval to the exact purchase plan. If the plan changes, approval must be earned again.
